I am a PhD researcher at **Delft University of Technology (TU Delft)** working on the **verification of learning-driven control in safety-critical systems**, as a part of the **Algorithmics group** in the **Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science (EEMCS)**.

I work in the **[SUMI Lab](https://annalukina.com/team/)** led by **Anna Lukina**, who is my PhD supervisor, and my promotor is **Matthijs T. J. Spaan**. My research focuses on verification of neural network-based controllers for dynamic and stochastic systems, with a particular interest in how formal guarantees can be maintained or reclaimed when systems change after verification. More broadly, I am interested in safe and reliable machine learning for control, certificate-based verification, and compositional methods.

**Contact:** You can reach me via my university email or LinkedIn.

# Research interests

- Verification of sequential decision making
- Certificate-based verification
- Stochastic and uncertain dynamical systems
- Safety-critical autonomous systems
- Logic for safe AI
- Neural network controllers
- Compositional verification and control
